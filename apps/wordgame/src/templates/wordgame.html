How to play
Place tiles from your rack onto the 15×15 board to form words, crossword style. The first word must cover the center star. New words must connect to tiles already on the board. Letter and word bonus squares multiply your score; use all 7 tiles in one turn for a 50-point bingo. Three consecutive passes end the game.
